# RDWG Research Data Infrastructure

Evidence base compiled by Tim Dennis (UCLA Library Data Science Center) to support the One IT Research Working Group (2026).

## About

The UCLA Research Data Working Group (RDWG) produced a roadmap in 2022 documenting infrastructure gaps, peer benchmarking, and recommendations for UCLA's research data ecosystem. This site surfaces that evidence base to inform the One IT Research Working Group, charged by the EVCP and CIO in 2026 to assess research technology infrastructure and produce recommendations.

## Contents

- **Gaps analysis** — UCLA's research data infrastructure gaps mapped against peer institutions
- **Peer evidence** — Benchmarking data from 25 R1 libraries and peer institution case studies
- **Risk patterns** — Documented risks from IT consolidation at peer institutions
- **Faculty voices** — Direct faculty testimony from 33 leadership meetings, 4 public forums, and Academic Senate sessions (2023)
- **UC policy** — UC Research Data Policy (2022) analysis and implications
- **Bibliography** — Full citation list in RIS format for import
